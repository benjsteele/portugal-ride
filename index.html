🍽️ Nutrition Plan
🔴 High Intensity — Days 1, 2 & 3  ·  70–90 g carbs/hour
Prioritise simple sugars. Avoid heavy fibre before and during riding. Why white rice/pasta? Brown rice and whole grains digest too slowly during high-intensity blocks. Save the fibre for rest days.

⚠️ Three Mistakes to Avoid
  1. New foods on hard days — Do not try a strange pastry before a climb. Stick to gels and bananas on Days 1–3.
  2. Skipping post-ride fuel — Your next day starts the moment you stop. That chocolate milk matters.
  3. Over-eating fibre — Beans, lentils, and whole grains cause mid-ride digestive distress at high intensity.
